# Jamaican Foodways — recipe provenance and publication gate

Updated 2026-10-16. STAGING ONLY. No merge or production publication authorized.

## Correction of prior editorial failure
The 107 named editorial recipe formulations were generated without sufficient recipe-level provenance or kitchen testing. Their inclusion in staging did not establish that their specific quantities or methods were used by Jamaican cooks. A dish's existence is not evidence for a particular formula.

## Enforced staging presentation
The Foodways landing page no longer exposes those formulas as visitor-ready recipes. Existing staged data and scripts remain preserved for research and audit; the recipes are not certified authentic. The new evidence-led landing section links directly to identified institutional references.

## Recipe-specific acceptance checklist (apply to EACH archived entry)
- Verify the named preparation is actually used by Jamaican cooks or communities, with a clear source.
- Trace exact ingredient list, proportions and steps to an attributable Jamaican cook/chef, cookbook or recorded practice. Distinguish source recipe from adaptations. Do not reproduce copyrighted prose.
- Check whether a source itself reports kitchen testing; if not, label the formula untested until independently prepared, evaluated and corrected.
- Record regional, family and period variation only with location, identity, date and appropriate permission.
- Audit food safety, especially ackee, cassava, kidney beans, meats, preservation, and allergens.
- Separate historical context from documented claims about the recipe's origin; add pinpoint references for contested claims.
- Obtain image permissions and captions before displaying third-party recipe photography.
