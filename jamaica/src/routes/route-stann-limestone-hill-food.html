Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Buy the cave visit from its actual operator

    Check whether admission, transport and optional products are included before payment. Do not accept a stranger’s claim that a separate unofficial escort is compulsory.

  • Use the farm’s booking channel and directions

    Confirm the sitting, cancellation terms, dietary needs and total with the host. An online photograph or map pin is not permission to arrive at a private farm.

  • Caves are guided environments

    Stay on the operator’s route, follow its equipment advice and do not enter an unstaffed cave or leave the group for photographs.
