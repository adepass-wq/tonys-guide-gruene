Choose how to travel

Walking
Use a short Savanna-la-Mar civic/market walk. The fort branch is optional and locally assessed; Blenheim is a separate vehicle visit.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 stays in Savanna-la-Mar. Day 2 makes the arranged Blenheim visit and returns. Interpret Frome through documents and a guide’s discussion rather than entering an operating factory.
Public transport & coach connections
Knutsford lists western passenger locations; confirm a bookable journey to Savanna-la-Mar or Lucea and arrange the town and hill transfers. A local fixed-route fare is different from whole-vehicle hire. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Ask for a short exterior civic route and seating. Ask Blenheim about its actual entrance and toilets. Skip the fort if the approach or damaged fabric is unsuitable.
Costs to check
Town guiding, food, local transport and any confirmed birthplace admission. JNHT’s older published tour prices are not treated as current quotes.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Do not buy factory access from a roadside offer

    This route includes no Frome factory tour. A historic account or development proposal does not grant entry to an operating industrial site.

  • Agree purchases before accepting them

    Ask the total for a meal, photograph, guiding or carrying bags before the service begins. Decline politely if you do not want it, and go to staffed premises if pressure escalates.

  • Ruins require a suitable approach

    Stay outside unstable walls and away from the sea edge. A host’s access check governs the fort branch; skip it if that cannot be established.
