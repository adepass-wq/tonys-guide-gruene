    St. Ann’s Bay town and Garvey connection

    Location: St. Ann’s Bay, Jamaica

  3. Day 1 · Stop 3

    St. Ann’s Bay shore and working landscape

    Location: St Ann's Bay Jamaica

    Observe the harbour from a permitted public position. Compare work, buildings and coast with historical material; request permission before photographing people at work.

    Getting there: Local branch chosen with your guide; no entry to an active wharf is implied.

    Drax Hall: food and a pause

    Address: Drax Hall, west of Ocho Rios

    Have a jerk meal and discuss preparation with the cook if the service allows. Leave the final block for an unhurried return rather than another distant attraction.

Choose how to travel

Walking
Walk the confirmed Seville visitor route and a short St. Ann’s Bay town branch. Transfer between the estate, town and Drax Hall.
Driving / hired driver
From Ocho Rios travel west to Seville, back to St. Ann’s Bay, then toward Drax Hall for the meal and return. Choose this branch instead of adding Nine Mile.
Public transport & coach connections
Book a coach to the passenger terminal serving your St. Ann base; confirm whether your ticket uses Drax Hall or another pickup. Arrange local licensed taxis for Seville and the town. Local coastal buses require stop and return confirmation. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗

Costs to check

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Verify Seville admission and date directly

    An unofficial escort fee does not establish estate access. Use the operator’s current weekday booking, agree transport separately and ask the total meal price and currency at the food stop.

  • Keep transport and payment clear

    Confirm licensed vehicle/driver identification, whether the quote is per person or whole vehicle, JMD or USD, admissions, waiting and return pickup. Decline unrequested services politely and seek help in staffed premises if pressure continues.
