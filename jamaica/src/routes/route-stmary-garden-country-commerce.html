Choose how to travel

Walking
Walk a short Annotto Bay branch, selected Castleton paths and a modest Highgate town section. There is no continuous pedestrian route between them.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 stays in Annotto Bay. Day 2 pairs Castleton with a driver-confirmed Highgate branch, or chooses only one inland destination if the transfer would rush the day.
Public transport & coach connections
Use Knutsford to Annotto Bay only when the current passenger booking confirms your journey. Ask locally about licensed buses or route taxis on each inland leg; a return arrangement is essential. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Check the church entrance and garden toilets, paths and staffing. Use a vehicle-assisted town visit if narrow pavements or slopes are unsuitable.
Costs to check
Local guiding and transport, meals and any organised garden visit; agree the cost of private vehicle waiting time.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Shared fare or private charter?

    Ask whether the quote is per person on a fixed route or for the whole vehicle. Confirm destination, currency and the last return before boarding; request the route and driver identification.

  • Permission is separate from guiding

    A street guide cannot grant church entry on the congregation’s behalf. Ask the actual host and decline an unsolicited access fee.

  • The garden river is not part of this outing

    Remain on the confirmed visitor paths and avoid floodwater or an unplanned river swim, especially after upstream rain.
