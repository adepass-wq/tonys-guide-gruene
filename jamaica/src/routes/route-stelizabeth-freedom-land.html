    Accompong community, by arrangement

    Location: Accompong St Elizabeth Jamaica

    Listen to the host’s account of governance, memory and present life, then compare it with the treaty essay. Visit only the sites included in the host’s actual programme.

    Getting there: Separate community day; confirm meeting point, access and the return driver.

  2. Day 2 · Stop 2

    Lacovia town and tombstones

    Location: Lacovia Tombstones St Elizabeth Jamaica

    Read the surviving inscriptions from a permitted position with local guidance. Separate the inscription from the colourful story about why the men died; an oft-repeated tale is not the same as evidence.

    Getting there: Short town branch with the approach and permission checked locally.

  3. Day 2 · Stop 3

    Holland Bamboo drive-through

    Address: Holland, near Lacovia, St. Elizabeth

    Observe the roadside landscape from the vehicle. Take a photo only at a lawful, fully off-road stopping place; there is no requirement to walk or stand in the traffic corridor.

    Getting there: Driver checks the current canopy, debris and road conditions; dated pictures are context.

    Middle Quarters pepper shrimp

    Address: Middle Quarters, St. Elizabeth

    Choose an operating vendor, confirm what is available and agree the total before buying. Let the meal connect the landscape day to contemporary work.

    Getting there: Final local food stop and prearranged return, rather than another distant excursion.

Choose how to travel

Walking
Walk Accompong with the community host and a modest Lacovia branch. Holland Bamboo is a live highway, not a pedestrian tunnel.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 centres the arranged Accompong visit and return. Day 2 follows a driver-confirmed Lacovia–Holland Bamboo–Middle Quarters branch from your actual accommodation.
Public transport & coach connections
Check whether a bookable Knutsford passenger stop such as Luana or Gutters works for your base. Arrange all rural transfers and return separately; no direct coach to Accompong or the roadside stops is promised. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Ask the community about slopes, surfaces and seated options. For Bamboo Avenue, photograph only if the driver can stop lawfully and fully clear of traffic.
Costs to check
Community hosting, local transport, meals and roadside food. Confirm each provider’s charge rather than assuming the first price includes the whole day.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • A real host and an agreed community fee

    Confirm who will meet you and what is included. A roadside offer of guaranteed festival, ritual or drumming access does not replace a community arrangement.

  • Bamboo Avenue is a road

    Do not stand in the carriageway for the perfect photograph or pay someone who waves you into an unsafe stop. Stay in the vehicle if a suitable stopping place is unavailable.

  • Menu, quantity, currency

    Ask how much shrimp is included and whether the price is JMD or USD before accepting the serving. Keep your purchase separate from an unsolicited guiding or parking charge.
