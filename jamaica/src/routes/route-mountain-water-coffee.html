    Gordon Town

    Location: Gordon Town St Andrew Jamaica

    Pause with your driver at a suitable public place. Look at the river valley and town connections; discuss water, hillside settlement and everyday travel rather than treating the town as scenery.

    Getting there: First mountain-road leg; stop only where the driver identifies legal, suitable parking.

    Irish Town: a coffee and meal pause

    Address: Irish Town, St. Andrew

    Use a confirmed cafe stop for coffee and a valley view. If you want cultivation and processing explained, arrange a named farm tour separately; buying a cup is not a farm visit.

    Getting there: Continue uphill by vehicle; confirm the cafe’s opening and whether a tour is included.

Choose how to travel

Walking
Walk at the booked coffee visit and on an appropriate Holywell trail. Do not walk the entire Gordon Town–Irish Town–Holywell road corridor.
Driving / hired driver
Use a mountain-experienced driver for Kingston → Gordon Town → Irish Town → Holywell and return. Ask about the road approach, rain and visibility before departure.
Public transport & coach connections
A coach can bring you to Kingston; it does not take this excursion into Holywell. Ask locally about buses or route taxis to Gordon Town, then arrange a confirmed onward vehicle and return. A driver is the simpler complete-day option. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗ · JUTC route guide ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Ask park management to identify the exact trail gradient, surface and toilets. A cafe view can replace a walk, but verify its approach and seating.
Costs to check
Allow for park entry, a booked coffee experience, food and a full-day vehicle quote.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • A mountain booking needs a mountain return

    Agree the actual coffee stop, park entrance and pickup with the driver. Do not follow a roadside claim of a cheaper private trail or farm visit without verifying permission and conditions.

  • Keep transport and payment clear

    Confirm licensed vehicle/driver identification, whether the quote is per person or whole vehicle, JMD or USD, admissions, waiting and return pickup. Decline unrequested services politely and seek help in staffed premises if pressure continues.
