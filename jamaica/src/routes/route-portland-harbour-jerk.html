Choose how to travel

Walking
Walk a modest Port Antonio/Titchfield branch with a local guide. Boston and Frenchman’s Cove require separate road transfers.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 stays in Port Antonio. Day 2 follows Port Antonio → Frenchman’s Cove → Boston Bay and returns; choose Reach Falls on another day.
Public transport & coach connections
Knutsford Express has a Port Antonio booking option; confirm the passenger arrival and transfer into town. On the coastal day, use a licensed driver or locally confirmed buses/route taxis with a planned return; no door-to-door coach excursion is implied. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Titchfield streets can be sloped and uneven. Ask the cove operator about its actual entrance, toilets and beach approach; sand is a separate barrier.
Costs to check
Local guiding/transfers, food, and cove or beach charges from the chosen operator.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Beach access, food and guiding are different charges

    Agree the named beach entrance, facilities and meal price before accepting the service. A coach terminal is not a beach pickup; get the local return in writing or a clear confirmed message.

  • Keep transport and payment clear

    Confirm licensed vehicle/driver identification, whether the quote is per person or whole vehicle, JMD or USD, admissions, waiting and return pickup. Decline unrequested services politely and seek help in staffed premises if pressure continues.
