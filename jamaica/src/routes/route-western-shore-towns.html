    Address: 1 White Hall Road, Negril

Choose how to travel

Walking
Walk a chosen beach segment and a short Lucea town branch. Beach, Negril town and Lucea are separate journeys.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 links a permitted Negril beach entrance with a town meal. Day 2 goes to Lucea and a prearranged Fort Charlotte branch; return by vehicle.
Public transport & coach connections
Book coach travel to Negril and confirm the terminal-to-lodging transfer. For Lucea, confirm the local licensed coastal service and return before setting out; a Negril coach ticket is not a Fort Charlotte tour. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Ask the beach host about the actual path and toilets. Lucea’s fort approach and town surfaces need separate confirmation; offer a town-only alternative.
Costs to check
Beach-host amenities if used, town meals, local guiding and transport. Confirm any fort visit terms.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Confirm the actual beach entrance and local fare

    Use a permitted entrance and check whether amenities cost extra. Ask whether a taxi quote is per person or whole vehicle, and arrange the Lucea/fort return before leaving your base.

  • Keep transport and payment clear

    Confirm licensed vehicle/driver identification, whether the quote is per person or whole vehicle, JMD or USD, admissions, waiting and return pickup. Decline unrequested services politely and seek help in staffed premises if pressure continues.
