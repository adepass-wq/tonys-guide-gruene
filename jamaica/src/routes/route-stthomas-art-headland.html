  1. Day 1 · Stop 1

    Yallahs town and a meal

    Use a short public town visit to connect agriculture, movement and the cultural histories in the guide. With a willing host, ask about local art and African continuities; do not request an impromptu rite as entertainment.

    Getting there: Meet the local guide at the agreed town point; ask before portraits or recordings.

  2. Day 1 · Stop 2

    Orange Park, only with a confirmed hosted visit

    If invited or booked, discuss the former coffee landscape and artists’ connections with the host. Without permission, read the article during the town meal; do not enter the property or stop on an unsafe road.

    Getting there: Conditional inland branch; verify the host, meeting point and return before leaving Yallahs.

  3. Day 2 · Stop 3

    Morant Point Lighthouse: a confirmed exterior branch

    If the custodian and driver confirm approach and access, examine the lighthouse setting from a permitted position. JNHT connects its construction with African Kru workers; use that account without treating the coast as empty scenery.

    Getting there: Remote vehicle leg with a current road/access check and daylight return. No tower climb is promised.

  4. Day 2 · Stop 4 · replacement option

    Bath Botanic Garden: replacement when the headland is unavailable

    Choose the garden instead of the remote branch when its caretaker confirms suitable access. Read botanical history and take a measured plant-and-landscape walk; this is a replacement, not an extra distant stop.

    Getting there: Alternative day-2 destination: arrange it separately and omit Morant Point if you choose it.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Permission comes from the host

    Do not pay an intermediary who claims to guarantee private-house entry or a Kumina ceremony. Confirm the host and agreed programme through a real venue/community contact.

  • No risky shortcut for a map pin

    Ask the driver and custodian about the headland approach. Do not enter floodwater, cross a locked gate or follow an unverified shortcut; take the garden alternative if access cannot be established.

  • Decline unrequested services before they start

    Agree any guide, bathing service or photo fee with the actual provider. If a supposed free favour becomes a demand, stop and seek help from named staff rather than arguing in an isolated place.
