Choose how to travel

Walking
Take separate guided town walks around Sam Sharpe Square and central Falmouth. Do not walk the inter-town coastal road.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 is downtown Montego Bay. Day 2 transfers to Falmouth, stays in its town branch and returns or uses a Falmouth overnight.
Public transport & coach connections
Check coach booking options for Montego Bay and Falmouth, including the exact passenger terminals. A terminal transfer is separate from the heritage walk. Locally confirmed buses can connect towns; leave room for waiting and a return. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Town paving, crossings and old interiors vary. Request a seated museum option and short exterior walking route; do not infer access from heritage designation.
Costs to check
Museum admission, named guiding, food and inter-town travel; interior tours may be separately charged.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • A closure claim deserves a direct check

    Confirm today’s museum and town-guide arrangement with the actual provider. Do not pay a stranger for supposed compulsory access to public streets or accept an unverified substitute tour under pressure.

  • Keep transport and payment clear

    Confirm licensed vehicle/driver identification, whether the quote is per person or whole vehicle, JMD or USD, admissions, waiting and return pickup. Decline unrequested services politely and seek help in staffed premises if pressure continues.
