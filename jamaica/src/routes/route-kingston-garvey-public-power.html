Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Verify the guide before following

    Arrange the guide through your museum, hotel or an independently checked operator. Agree the meeting point, duration and total before the walk; an unsolicited escort is not your booking.

  • A public building does not need an unofficial admission fixer

    If someone claims a building is closed or demands a special access fee, check with the named institution directly. Pay its actual desk or official booking channel. Keep the library appointment separate from street offers.
