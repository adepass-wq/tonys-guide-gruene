    Middle Quarters pepper shrimp

    Buy from an operating vendor and ask about preparation when the work allows. Make the food itself the encounter; do not assume a universal recipe or a scheduled demonstration.

    Getting there: Separate road journey on the food day; stop where the driver can park suitably.

Choose how to travel

Walking
Take a short Black River town walk and brief vendor stops. Town, river operator and food districts require arranged transfers.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 pairs Black River town with a named river operator. Day 2 travels to Middle Quarters and Border/Scott’s Cove, then returns to your base. Ask the driver for the practical order from your accommodation.
Public transport & coach connections
Ask Knutsford Express which bookable passenger stop serves your base; use a confirmed local transfer from that stop. Do not assume a direct coach to Black River, Treasure Beach or either vendor district. Licensed local buses/route taxis need current route and return confirmation. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Ask the boat operator about boarding, seating, lifejackets and toilets. Roadside stalls vary in surfaces and seating; arrange a vehicle-assisted stop.
Costs to check
Named river tour, transport, two food purchases and meals. Vendors and operators set their own current prices.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Book the boat; agree the serving

    Check the river operator, boarding arrangements and inclusions directly. At food stalls confirm quantity, currency and total before buying; a parking or guiding favour you never requested is not part of your order.

  • Keep transport and payment clear

    Confirm licensed vehicle/driver identification, whether the quote is per person or whole vehicle, JMD or USD, admissions, waiting and return pickup. Decline unrequested services politely and seek help in staffed premises if pressure continues.
