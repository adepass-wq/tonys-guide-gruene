Choose how to travel

Walking
Use separate short walks in Oracabessa and Port Maria. The coast between them is a vehicle journey.
Driving / hired driver
Follow Oracabessa → Port Maria; add Firefly only with confirmed access and enough time for its separate uphill approach.
Public transport & coach connections
Check a bookable coach journey to Port Maria or your actual nearest passenger stop; arrange the terminal transfer. Licensed coastal route taxis may serve town legs after local confirmation. Firefly needs its own arranged return. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Ask about pavement, shade, toilets and the house’s steps. Use a vehicle-assisted exterior town route if a full walk is unsuitable.
Costs to check
Town viewing, a meal, local transfers and Firefly admission if booked; agree driver waiting time.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • A famous house is still a separate booking

    Confirm Firefly directly before paying an intermediary. Keep town streets and public viewpoints separate from private-house entry; retain the return driver for the uphill branch.

  • Keep transport and payment clear

    Confirm licensed vehicle/driver identification, whether the quote is per person or whole vehicle, JMD or USD, admissions, waiting and return pickup. Decline unrequested services politely and seek help in staffed premises if pressure continues.
