Choose how to travel

Walking
Walk only the community host’s agreed route. Buff Bay, Charles Town and Moore Town are separate road journeys.
Driving / hired driver
Day 1 pairs Buff Bay with the confirmed Charles Town visit. Day 2 visits Moore Town from a suitable base. The two communities are not interchangeable and need separate introductions.
Public transport & coach connections
A confirmed coach to Port Antonio or another actual passenger stop is an arrival option. Arrange the rural leg and return with a licensed driver; the coach ticket is not a Maroon-community tour. Check coach journeys ↗ · JTB transport guidance ↗
Access and a shorter alternative
Ask the host about paths, slopes, seating and toilets. A seated conversation can replace a longer community walk.
Costs to check
Host/guide arrangements, separate local transport and meals. Agree community fees and any recording permission before the visit.

Safety and keeping control of the bill

  • Confirm the person who will host you

    Use a community or independently verified venue contact. Agree the programme, fee and meeting place in advance; a driver or roadside seller cannot promise a ceremony on the community’s behalf.

  • Ask before you record

    Permission to visit is not permission to photograph graves, record a speaker or post a performance. Accept a refusal without bargaining.

  • Keep the rural return booked

    Plan daylight transfers and retain your driver’s number. Do not rely on an unverified onward ride when a community visit finishes.
