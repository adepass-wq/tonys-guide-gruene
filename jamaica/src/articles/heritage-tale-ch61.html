The Fasting Trial (fragment).

A brief fasting contest is preserved with two sets of song words.

← Written talesJamaican voices →

Teller and place: Julia Gentle, Santa Cruz Mountains.

Reading note: Hunger; the source labels this a fragment.

Edition: collected during Beckwith’s 1919/1921 visits; published 1924. Historical transcription, not a newly composed retelling. Narrative wording and printed song words retained; page and reference markers removed. This text is not a transcript of the modern performances in the listening room.

Jumpin’ Dick say he would bear longer hunger than White Belly. So White Belly up a tree where a grape a drop, an’ Jumpin’ Dick pick up on de ground.

White Belly say,

“Coo coo, me lovin’! coo coo, me lovin’!

Everybody goin’ to look for dem dandy.”

Jumpin’ Dick dance an’ sing,

“Every Monday morning,

Zum zick a zum zum!

Every Monday morning,

Zum zick a zum zum!”
