More than instructions

Origins without myths

Food histories require evidence. Trace Indigenous ingredients and knowledge, African continuities, plantation-era economies, indenture and trade without claiming that a modern recipe remained unchanged for centuries.

Family and parish variations

Compare ingredients, techniques and names only when a community source can identify them. Family testimony should name the cook, location, date and consent wherever possible.

Food and travel

Pair a recipe with real-world markets and eating places through the existing food directory and the regional guides.
