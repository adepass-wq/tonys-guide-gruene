Before you go: transport, access and changing conditions

Coach travel connects bases. It does not automatically include the local journey to an attraction. Book the actual origin, destination and date, confirm the passenger terminal, then arrange the local leg and return. A courier office alone does not establish a passenger departure.

For local public transport, confirm the licensed route, pickup, fare, last return and any transfer with a local provider. These routes do not invent bus numbers or timetables. A hired driver is recommended where several remote stops would otherwise depend on uncertain onward rides.

Choose a short town branch if heat, slopes or uneven surfaces make a long walk unsuitable. Ask the venue about your actual entrance, route, toilets and assistance. Public heritage designation does not grant entry to private property or establish wheelchair access.

TRAVEL WITH A PLAN · KEEP YOUR BOUNDARIES

Agree the journey and the price before it begins.

Confirm your transport

For a route taxi, the Transport Authority specifies red PPV plates, marked route details and Route Taxi doors, with driver identification and badge. Check the actual driver and licensed route as well as the vehicle. A separately charged shared-route seat is different from a private vehicle hire.

Make the quote complete

Ask: JMD or USD? Per person or whole vehicle? One way or return? How much waiting? Which entrances, food and optional activities are included? What is the cancellation rule? Save the agreed details and use the venue’s or operator’s actual payment channel.

For a meal, agree portion or fish weight, preparation and total first. Ask whether the bill already includes a service charge before choosing a tip.

Decline without a debate

“No thank you, I don’t need a guide.” “I’m checking with the venue directly.” “We agreed this total; please show the bill.” Do not accept a service you do not want just to end a conversation. A friendly stranger is not automatically dishonest; a claimed compulsory fee still deserves a direct check.

If pressure becomes threatening, move toward staffed premises and ask for help. Prioritise personal safety over a disputed payment or belongings.

Keep the return and conditions in view

Arrange rural return transport, charge your phone and share the driver and expected return with someone you trust. Ask your host about roads and river conditions, travel unfamiliar rural roads in daylight and avoid floodwater. A photograph or Google map cannot inspect today’s entrance or trail.
