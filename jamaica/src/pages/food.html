Make the meal work for your visit

A fast meal still needs a little margin

Counter-service food is useful for a short port call. Ask what is ready, check the queue and keep your return time in view. A nearby branch is often a better decision than crossing town for a familiar brand.

Before a larger order

Confirm currency, tax, service and any weight-based seafood pricing. Ask about dietary restrictions directly; a vegetarian dish on a menu is not proof of an allergen-safe kitchen.

For dinner or a group

Reserve when the venue requests it, confirm the branch and settle your return ride. Restaurant opening, menu and live entertainment can change independently.
