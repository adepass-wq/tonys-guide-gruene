Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Choose a guided raft rather than an inner tube. Calypso’s published ticket covers one or two people per raft. Agree what the ticket includes, life-jacket provision, your meeting point and the return transfer before booking. The same operator currently suspends tubing; that does not mean its raft trip is suspended.
