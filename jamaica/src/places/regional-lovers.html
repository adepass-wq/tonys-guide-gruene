Why make room for it?

JTB locates Lovers’ Leap at Yardley Chase near Southfield. The well-known lovers’ account belongs to folklore; do not turn it into a documented biography simply because it is attached to a dramatic landscape.

Look, listen and ask

Listen to how the story is told and compare it with the visible geography. Stay within designated visitor areas, and ask about conditions if wind or weather affects the lookout.

Tony’s call
Choose the view and a locally paced visit; do not squeeze it between a long safari and distant falls.
