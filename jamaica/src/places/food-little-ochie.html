Why make room for it?

JTB identifies Little Ochie in Alligator Pond, Manchester, among South Coast seafood stops. Its geography is part of the choice: use the coastal village as the destination rather than a generic search result that may label it with the better-known inland town.

Look, listen and ask

Ask what is available, how it will be cooked and what the final portion costs. Keep working boats and fish handling areas clear. A meal can introduce the fishing landscape without treating local people as part of a staged attraction.

Tony’s call

Best for

Skip if

Time — planning estimate
Allow 90 minutes or more as a meal-planning estimate; ask about the day’s queue.
Cost
Ask what is priced by weight or portion, what sides cost, currency and any service charges before ordering.
Hours & booking
Confirm current opening, seafood availability and group reservations with the restaurant.
Access
Ask about parking-to-table surfaces, beach sand, seating and toilets; waterfront dining does not establish accessible entry.
Parking & pickup
Arrange the exact entrance and pickup with your host, driver or venue; directions are destination-based, not a surveyed route.
