Read Tony’s guide & practical details

JNHT places the historic courthouse opposite Claude Stuart Park and the parish church. Use that physical arrangement to read the town: who administered it, who worshipped there, and how a coastal settlement connected people and commerce. These are active community spaces. Exterior viewing and a quiet public-street walk are different from access to civic offices or a church interior; request permission rather than assuming a visitor route has opened every door.

Tony’s Call One of the best places to bring the St. Mary history off the screen. Arrange a knowledgeable local guide if you want more than photographs of attractive buildings.
Best for
History, architecture and visitors interested in a working town beyond resorts.
Skip if
Your priority is beach facilities or a packaged amusement venue.
Timing
Allow 1–2 hours in daylight; planning estimate.
Cost
Public streets/exterior viewing do not require a sightseeing ticket; guides and interior access arrangements may cost extra.
Hours & booking
Institutional access by arrangement; avoid interrupting worship or official business.
Accessibility
Pavements, road crossings and historic building steps vary; request a short vehicle-assisted route if needed.
Parking & arrival
Use legal parking agreed locally; do not block civic access or busy roadside shops.
Sensible next stop
Firefly if its opening is confirmed, or Oracabessa on the return west.
