Why make room for it?

JTB describes Rocklands as a sanctuary near Anchovy with native and migratory birds and hummingbird feeding. Wildlife is the attraction, but the most useful skill to take home is observation: colour, movement, shape and sound, rather than a promise of a photograph with a bird.

Look, listen and ask

Follow staff directions about feeding, movement and flash. Try recording three field notes—one visual detail, one sound and one behaviour—before identifying the bird. Ask which birds are resident and which arrive seasonally.

Tony’s call
Make birds the purpose of the outing. Choose this over a busy attraction package when your group enjoys patient observation.
Best for
Birdwatchers and visitors who want a quieter hillside experience.
Skip if
You expect a guaranteed encounter with a particular species or a fully verified step-free route.
Time — planning estimate
Allow 60–90 minutes on site as an estimate, plus your actual road transfer.
Cost
Get the current sanctuary admission separately from any transfer package.
Hours & booking
Confirm opening and the feeding/guide arrangements directly before travelling.
Access
Ask about the path from vehicle to seating, uneven surfaces, toilets and assistance; a seated activity does not establish accessible entry.
Parking & pickup
Arrange the exact entrance and pickup with your host, driver or venue; directions are destination-based, not a surveyed route.
Next stop
Choose an Anchovy-area return route; do not stack another distant hill property without checking transfers.
