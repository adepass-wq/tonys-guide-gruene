Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The tourism board lists Sun Valley Plantation Tours & Trails and includes it on its open-attractions list. Contact the actual operator to establish what is growing, what the visit includes and whether tastings are part of the day. Avoid relying on an old published farm-tour price. A productive landscape is not a neutral tropical garden: ask about farmers, crops, markets and changes in the local economy.

Tony’s Call A useful alternative to another adrenaline stop if you want to understand plants and livelihoods. Book it deliberately and ask what you will actually see.
Best for
Families, gardeners and visitors interested in food production.
Skip if
You want to arrive without an appointment or need an unverified accessible trail.
Timing
Ask the operator for tour duration; provisionally allow 1½–2 hours.
Cost
Current tour and tasting price needs an operator quote; do not budget from an undated US$12 guidebook figure.
Hours & booking
Arrange the date and meeting point through the listed operator.
Accessibility
Ask about slopes, muddy paths, seating and restrooms; trail accessibility is not verified.
Parking & arrival
Get the operator’s entrance/pickup instructions; a plantation name can cover a broad area.
Sensible next stop
Oracabessa and a relaxed coastal meal.
