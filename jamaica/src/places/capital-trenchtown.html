Why make room for it?

JTB’s 2026 Kingston guide describes the tenement yards and rooms connected with Bob Marley, and the current status table lists Culture Yard open. The useful visit is more than a celebrity photograph: ask the host how communal housing, music and neighbourhood life connect. Arrange the museum visit and transport rather than assuming an independent walk covers the hosted experience.

Tony’s call
Make room for a hosted explanation of the yard and its community history.
Best for
Visitors interested in music’s social setting as well as biography.
Skip if
You want an unarranged performance or unrestricted access to neighbouring homes.
Time — planning estimate
Allow 60–90 minutes for an agreed visit, plus the actual transfer; editorial estimate.

Hours & booking
Confirm the actual date, host or operator, meeting point and last entry; opening status is not a booking.
Access
Ask about the exact path, steps, surfaces, toilets and physical requirements.
Parking & pickup
Agree the actual entrance, lawful stopping or parking, and a pickup point with the operator or driver.
