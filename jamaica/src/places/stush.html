Stush in the Bush

Address: Free Hill, St. Ann; directions from host after booking

A reservation-only, plant-based farm dining experience that deserves its own afternoon and a serious budget.

Book for host’s directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The operator publishes Friday and Sunday visits at 1 p.m., a fully plant-based menu and a 4–5-hour experience. This is not a casual restaurant where you can arrive for a single dish. Book first; the host supplies directions and explicitly asks guests not to use Google Maps. The farm approach may require a shuttle for an unsuitable vehicle.

Tony’s Call A distinctive choice if food, farming and hospitality are the main event. Commit to the reservation and follow the host’s arrival instructions; do not squeeze it between two major excursions.
Best for
Plant-based dining, celebrations and visitors who want an extended farm experience.
Skip if
You need a low-cost lunch, a Saturday visit or a flexible walk-in stop.
Timing
Operator advises 4–5 hours on site, plus transport.
Cost
Published standard price US$227.50 per person; one price for all guests. Confirm extras and deposit/cancellation terms at booking.
Hours & booking
Published regular visits Friday/Sunday at 1 p.m.; always closed Saturdays. Private arrangements require separate agreement.
Accessibility
Farm surfaces can be muddy; request specific mobility, seating and shuttle arrangements when booking.
Parking & arrival
Host gives directions after booking; call at arrival and do not drive down the farm road without instructions.
Sensible next stop
Keep the rest of the afternoon free; a short coastal stop beforehand only if arrival time remains secure.
