Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Boston’s jerk shops are an important commercial expression of a wider Jamaican cooking tradition. Ask what is on the pit, what fuel is used and which portions are ready. The meal and Boston Bay’s surf setting are two different experiences: a food stop does not establish safe swimming or surfing conditions.

Tony’s call
Make Boston the meal stop on your Portland coastal day. Ask about preparation rather than treating every jerk pit as identical.
Best for
Jerk pork or chicken and a conversation about the cooking.
Consider another option if
You require a guaranteed menu or intend to fit Boston, rafting and Reach Falls into one rushed transfer day.
Time
Allow 45–90 minutes for a meal; editorial estimate, excluding the drive.
Cost
Compare the cook shop’s current menu and agree portion, sides and currency.
Hours & booking
Confirm the chosen cook shop’s service that day.
Access
Ask the chosen stall about steps, seating and toilets; beach access is separate.
Parking & pickup
Agree a safe stop and return pickup near the chosen cook shop.
Next stop
Frenchman’s Cove or Port Antonio if that suits your coastal route; Reach Falls is a separate eastern branch.
