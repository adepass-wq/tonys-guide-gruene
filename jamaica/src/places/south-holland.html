Why make room for it?

JTB currently lists Holland Bamboo open. The attraction is a planted avenue along a working road, not a closed pedestrian garden. Read the historical Bamboo Avenue photograph beside the present landscape, and ask your driver to choose lawful stopping and a suitable viewpoint instead of blocking traffic for a picture.

Tony’s call
Appreciate the avenue as part of a South Coast route; do not turn the carriageway into a photo studio.
