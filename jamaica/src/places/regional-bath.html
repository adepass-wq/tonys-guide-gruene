Why make room for it?

JIS identifies the Bath Fountain agency in St. Thomas. Treat this as a named destination with services to verify, rather than relying on a generic map pin for “healing water.” Tourism interest and medical effectiveness are separate questions.

Look, listen and ask

Before a visit, decide whether your interest is the town, the spring setting or a booked service. For any health-related suitability question, obtain appropriate clinical advice rather than taking a tourism claim as a prescription.
