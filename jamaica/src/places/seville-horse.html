Chukka Seville Horseback Ride & Swim

A horseback ride and sea swim from Chukka’s Seville outpost, west of Ocho Rios.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Chukka’s Seville outpost combines a trail ride with a sea portion when conditions allow. Its published horse-only requirements include minimum age six and maximum weight 230 lb. Closed-toe footwear is required; pregnancy and wheelchair access are excluded. Arrange the return transfer separately unless your booking explicitly includes it.
