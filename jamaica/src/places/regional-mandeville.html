Why make room for it?

JNHT identifies the courthouse as a Georgian building dating to 1817, shortly after the town’s incorporation. Use that dated landmark to orient yourself, then look beyond it: a historic square remains part of a functioning town, where shopping, work and transport shape the visitor’s route.

Look, listen and ask

Notice which features still serve public functions and which have changed use. A guide can connect the square to the wider Manchester landscape without promising entry to every old building.
