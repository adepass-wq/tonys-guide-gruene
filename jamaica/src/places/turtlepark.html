Turtle River Park

A central green-space break between the bay, shopping and lunch—distinct from Turtle River Falls & Gardens.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

UDC announced the park’s reopening in November 2025, and the tourism board lists it open. Use it as a place to slow down, regroup and take a short break in town. Do not confuse it with a paid waterfall attraction with a similar name. Nor should a proposal for daily reggae performances be treated as a confirmed daily entertainment schedule. Ask about any actual programme on the day.

Tony’s Call An easy component of a modest town day. The value is a pause in the middle of Ochi, not another attraction you must spend hours completing.
Best for
Families, short-stay visitors and people who want to keep the day local.
Skip if
You want a waterfall climb or a promised daily concert.
Timing
Allow 30–60 minutes; editorial estimate.
Cost
Confirm any current access or event charge with UDC/park staff; do not assume all special events share normal access terms.
Hours & booking
Confirm current park opening and any special-event restrictions.
Accessibility
Ask about the continuous paved route and restroom access. Avoid assigning a verified wheelchair rating without a site check.
Parking & arrival
Use legal town parking; confirm an easy drop-off instead of stopping in traffic.
Sensible next stop
Ocho Rios Bay Beach, craft shopping and Miss T’s Kitchen.
