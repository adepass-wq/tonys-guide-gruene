Why make room for it?

JTB identifies Boston’s beach as one of Jamaica’s surfing places and currently lists Boston Beach open. The waves that attract surfers can make this a poor choice for a group expecting quiet swimming. Choose a coached surf session only after agreeing the instructor, conditions and equipment; Boston jerk is a nearby food choice, not an included beach package.

Choose Boston for the character of this coast; do not judge swimming conditions by an open-attraction listing.
