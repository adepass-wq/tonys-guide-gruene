Why make room for it?

The Sands gives visitors a specific way to eat at The Caves without assuming every dining room in an all-inclusive property admits outsiders. The operator describes public jerk evenings on Wednesday and Saturday. Private cave dining is a different, separately arranged product.

Tony’s call
Choose the confirmed public jerk evening; do not turn a hotel name into a promise of unrestricted resort access.
Best for
A scheduled West End jerk evening.
Skip if
You want an any-night walk-in or expect private cave dining to be included.
Time — planning estimate
60–90 minutes; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Operator-listed public jerk evenings Wednesday and Saturday; confirm this week’s service, reservation and arrival rules.
Access
Ask about the actual public dining area, entrance steps and toilets; cave access is a separate question.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
Next stop
Use a confirmed West End pickup and return route.
