Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The operator offers the Sky Explorer, bobsled, Ragamuffin, zipline and other activities through packages. Read the package inclusions and rider restrictions before choosing. A multigenerational group may want different combinations, and a garden or viewing experience does not mean every ride is appropriate for everyone. This is engineered adventure in a forest setting, with queues and equipment procedures, rather than an independent hike.

Tony’s Call Good for a ride-focused family day. Compare local and visitor packages carefully, including identification requirements and the age cutoffs used by the operator.
Best for
Families, ride enthusiasts and groups who want an organised activity venue.
Skip if
You want a quiet nature walk or will use only a fraction of an expensive bundle.
Timing
Operator’s local packages list about 3 or 4 hours, depending on selection.
Cost
Published local Brawta: J$7,000 ages 10+, J$5,000 ages 5–9; local ID required. Larger local package: J$9,500 ages 10+. Visitor prices use separate checkout; verify taxes and current offers.
Hours & booking
Reserve for your date and check operating hours, weather closures and last ride entry.
Accessibility
Ride-specific height/age and transfer requirements apply. Ask which experiences accommodate your mobility needs before purchasing.
Parking & arrival
Confirm parking or package pickup; a transfer-inclusive excursion and gate ticket are different products.
Sensible next stop
Ocho Rios Bay Beach or a relaxed town meal, rather than a second major attraction.
