Puerto Seco Beach

Location: Puerto Seco Beach, Discovery Bay, Jamaica

A destination beach venue in Discovery Bay, suited to spending a substantial part of the day in one place.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The operator markets beach facilities, dining and watersports, while the tourism board identifies its floating aqua park. Check exactly what your entrance package includes: a beach day, activity pass and animal encounter are different purchases. Families should ask about age limits, flotation requirements and supervision for the aqua park. It is a more structured beach outing than simply finding a stretch of sand.

Tony’s Call Choose it when facilities and a full beach day outweigh the drive. Pair with Green Grotto only if you still leave enough unhurried beach time.
Best for
Families, groups and visitors who want a beach venue with organised facilities.
Skip if
You want a low-spend quick dip near Ocho Rios or dislike commercial beach settings.
Timing
Allow 3–5 hours for a beach day; planning estimate.
Cost
Paid access/packages. Confirm resident and visitor categories, equipment, rentals and optional activities directly.
Hours & booking
Check the operator’s date-specific opening and activity availability.
Accessibility
Ask about entrance-to-beach surfaces, toilets, shade and any beach mobility assistance. Water activities have their own physical demands.
Parking & arrival
Confirm venue parking and the named entrance at Discovery Bay.
Sensible next stop
Green Grotto Caves; Sharkies in Salem when travelling east.
