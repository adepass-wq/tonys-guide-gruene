Sharkies Seafood in Salem

A beachside seafood meal on the Runaway Bay corridor, suited to a western St. Ann day.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Sharkies’ operator gives its location as A1 Main Street, Salem, Runaway Bay. That matters because the brand also has a Kingston venue; book the north-coast branch you intend to visit. Check how a fish order is priced—by portion, weight or preparation—and agree extras before ordering. The beach setting is a reason to leave enough daylight for an unhurried meal, not proof that swimming or every waterfront facility is included. The image is St. Ann’s Bay coastal context.

Tony’s Call Use it as the food anchor of a Green Grotto or Puerto Seco day. Confirm the branch, menu and pricing basis before ordering a large seafood spread.
Best for
Seafood lovers and groups already travelling toward Runaway Bay.
Skip if
Seafood allergies or a strict short return window make an unhurried meal unsuitable.
Timing
Allow 1–1½ hours; planning estimate.
Cost
Menu/order-based. Ask whether fish is weight-priced, which sides are included and how service/tax are charged.
Hours & booking
Confirm current service and reserve for a group.
Accessibility
Ask about entrance and sand/paved seating, toilets and suitable tables. A beach restaurant is not automatically wheelchair-accessible.
Parking & arrival
Confirm on-site parking/drop-off through the Salem branch.
Sensible next stop
Puerto Seco and Green Grotto westward; Ochi on the return east.
