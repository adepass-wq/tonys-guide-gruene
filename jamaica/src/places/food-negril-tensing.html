Why make room for it?

The Lodge publishes seasonal Caribbean cooking and advance arrangements for vegetarian, vegan, dairy-free and gluten-free preferences. The operator also discloses three large Rhodesian Ridgebacks roaming the property. That matters to the choice as much as the menu: tell the restaurant about both dietary and access needs before reserving.

Time — planning estimate
90–120 minutes for dinner; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Operator-listed daily 10 a.m.–9 p.m.; reservations strongly recommended. Confirm current outside-guest and age policies.
Access
Ask about paths, steps, table placement and toilets; advise staff about mobility needs and dogs.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
