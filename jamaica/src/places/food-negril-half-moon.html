Why make room for it?

Half Moon Beach in Green Island offers Jamaican and international food in a beach setting. It is not the Half Moon resort in Rose Hall, St. James. Ask what seafood is available and whether your intended meal visit includes beach use; a restaurant booking and a day-beach arrangement can have different charges.

Tony’s call
Choose this when Green Island is your branch of the western coast; avoid treating every place marketed near Negril as the same location.

Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Operator-listed daily 9 a.m.–9 p.m.; confirm food service, beach access and any seasonal seafood availability.
Access
Ask about parking, sand, seating and toilets; request the specific route needed by your group.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
