Middle Quarters — pepper shrimp

A St. Elizabeth roadside food stop known for bags of pepper shrimp, useful on an inland route toward YS Falls.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The Jamaica Tourist Board identifies Middle Quarters with its roadside pepper-shrimp vendors. Choose a seller, ask how hot the batch is and agree the bag size and price. The stop belongs to a working town and its local food economy; ask permission before photographing the cook or stall.

Tony’s call
Pair a short shrimp stop with a journey already passing Middle Quarters. Keep the road stop separate from your booked YS Falls visit.
Best for
A spicy local snack on a South Coast or YS Falls route.
Consider another option if
Shellfish is unsuitable for your group or you need a reliably mild meal.
Time

Next stop
