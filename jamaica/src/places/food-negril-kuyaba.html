Why make room for it?

Kuyaba describes covered dining and an open-air wooden deck, with Jamaican breakfasts, seafood, pasta and choices for mixed groups. Decide whether you need shade and a straightforward seating route or want the more elevated setting. The gazebo tower and beach-side dining are different access questions.

Tony’s call
A sensible mixed-group meal on the beach strip; choose the table before assuming the setting suits everyone.
Best for
Groups balancing Jamaican cooking with broader menu preferences.
Skip if
Your group cannot use steps and the restaurant cannot confirm a suitable table route.
Time — planning estimate
60–90 minutes; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
The operator lists daily 7 a.m.–11 p.m.; reconfirm the particular meal service before visiting.
Access
Ask separately about the covered room, deck and tower seating, plus parking-to-table surfaces.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
