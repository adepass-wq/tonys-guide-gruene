Island Village shopping and boardwalk

A walkable shopping, food and boardwalk cluster near the main cruise-terminal side of town.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Start with the boardwalk, then choose food, crafts or coffee without arranging another long road transfer. This is a useful cluster for a short port stay, but the Reynolds Pier berth has a different approach. Ask terminal staff for the designated pedestrian route, then check each tenant’s hours.
