Why make room for it?

National Gallery West is the National Gallery of Jamaica’s western branch at the Cultural Centre on Sam Sharpe Square. The setting makes this more than a rainy-day substitute: contemporary art and the surrounding civic landscape invite questions about whose lives are represented in the city.

Look, listen and ask

Choose one artwork and one feature of the square. What does each ask you to remember? Read the full Sam Sharpe and Burchell histories in the guide before deciding that the buildings tell the whole story.

Tony’s call
Start indoors with the exhibits, then read the square through what you have learned. Downtown deserves more than a drive-through.
Best for
Readers interested in Jamaican art, civic history and the meaning of public monuments.

Ask for current museum/gallery hours, exhibition access and any group reservation.

Arrange the exact entrance and pickup with your host, driver or venue; directions are destination-based, not a surveyed route.
