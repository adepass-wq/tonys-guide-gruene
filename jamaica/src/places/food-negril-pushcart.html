Why make room for it?

Pushcart is Rockhouse’s casual Jamaican-food venue, distinct from the property’s more composed restaurant. Food, rum-bar atmosphere and music make this a social choice. Choose by the evening you want, not simply by finding two restaurants with the same hotel address.

Tony’s call
Choose Pushcart for jerk and atmosphere; choose Rockhouse Restaurant when the meal and conversation are the main event.
Best for
Informal West End evenings and Jamaican cooking.
Skip if
Music or a lively bar atmosphere would spoil the meal.
Time — planning estimate
60–90 minutes; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
JTB currently lists Pushcart open; confirm that evening’s food service and any music programme with Rockhouse.
Access
Confirm entrance-to-table steps and the exact seating area; a sea view is not a step-free rating.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
Next stop
Keep the rest of the evening on the West End and arrange the return ride.
