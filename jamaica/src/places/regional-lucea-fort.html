Why make room for it?

JNHT locates Fort Charlotte in Lucea and describes its eighteenth-century defensive purpose. The useful question is not simply how old the walls are: why did this harbour need defence, and how did military power sit alongside the working town?

Look, listen and ask

Read the Lucea and Fort Charlotte histories before visiting. Identify the relationship between harbour, town and defensive site from permitted viewpoints. Do not enter restricted grounds or climb masonry to improve a photograph.

Public streets and any guided/site access have different costs. Ask for a full local guide quote.
Hours & booking
Arrange site access locally or with JNHT; designation is not a promise of walk-in entry.
Access
Confirm lawful entry, road approach, steps, edge protection, toilets and a suitable place to wait.
Parking & pickup
Arrange the exact entrance and pickup with your host, driver or venue; directions are destination-based, not a surveyed route.
