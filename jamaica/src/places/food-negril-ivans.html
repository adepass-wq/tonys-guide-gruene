Why make room for it?

Ivan’s gives the West End another meal-led option. The operator welcomes visitors and publishes distinct breakfast, lunch and dinner periods. Choose a table and service deliberately; a cliff-front restaurant is a different experience from eating on the sandy beach strip.

Tony’s call
Make a sunset reservation the evening’s anchor and sort the ride home before arriving.
Best for
A planned West End dinner and diners who want a named restaurant reservation.
Skip if
Cliff-side steps or the seating route do not fit your group’s access needs.
Time — planning estimate
90–120 minutes for a dinner; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Operator-listed breakfast 7 a.m.–noon, lunch noon–4 p.m., dinner 4–10 p.m. JTB lists open; confirm the reservation and current service.
Access
Request the exact entrance, table route, steps, edge setting and toilets before booking.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
Next stop
Keep the evening on the West End; do not assume beach and cliff restaurants are adjacent.
