Why make room for it?

The operator and Jamaica Tourism Board describe RastaSafari as an ATV-based countryside experience around Roaring River in Westmoreland. JTB currently lists it open. It is separately operated from Roaring River Park and Cave, which the same status table marks closed. Agree the riding role, age and physical requirements, food, transfers and any river participation before paying; the social-media contact page may require sign-in.

Tony’s call
Treat it as the day’s main inland outing, with the actual transfer included in your plan.
Best for
A hosted countryside encounter with a clearly agreed programme.
Skip if
You do not want a motorised outing, or cannot establish that the riding and physical requirements fit your group.
Time — planning estimate
Allow a half-day including your actual transfer; agree the operator’s programme length. Editorial estimate.
Cost
Confirm the current admission, chosen programme and any separately charged transport, guiding or equipment.
Hours & booking
Confirm the actual date, host or operator, meeting point and last entry; opening status is not a booking.
Access
Ask about the exact path, steps, surfaces, toilets and physical requirements.
Parking & pickup
Agree the actual entrance, lawful stopping or parking, and a pickup point with the operator or driver.
Next stop
