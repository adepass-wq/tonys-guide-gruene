Island Gully Falls / Blue Hole

Location: Island Gully Falls, Thatch Hill Road, Jamaica

Find on Google Maps ↗

Deep blue river pools and cascades in the hills, with guided water activities and a more physical outing than a town beach.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

“Blue Hole” is a widely used name, so confirm Island Gully Falls and its Thatch Hill Road entrance when arranging transport. The attraction describes guided visits, swimming and rope swings; the tourism board lists changing facilities, lockers and life jackets. You do not have to jump because someone else does. Agree your comfort level with the guide, and ask about river conditions before entering. Clear weather at your hotel does not establish conditions upstream.

Tony’s Call Choose this for the river, not for a checklist of jumps. A sensible guide respects a visitor who declines an activity.
Best for
Confident water participants and families who can manage uneven ground.
Skip if
You need a reliably step-free route or prefer a supervised pool to a natural river.
Timing
Allow 1½–2½ hours plus your transfer; planning estimate.
Cost
Official tickets advertise prices starting at US$6.50, not a universal adult rate. Select your actual age/residency category before treating it as your budget.
Hours & booking
Operator publishes Sunday–Friday, 8:30 a.m.–4:30 p.m.; reconfirm Saturday availability rather than assuming daily opening.
Accessibility
Rocky trails, bridges and water entry; request assistance and life-jacket fit. A complete step-free route is not verified.
Parking & arrival
Arrange a named entrance, parking and return pickup with your driver; several nearby businesses use similar names.
Sensible next stop
Miss T’s Kitchen back in Ocho Rios; avoid adding another strenuous waterfall solely to fill the day.
