Why make room for it?

Choose this when you want a meal woven into a Seven Mile Beach day. Skylark’s location gives you a different setting from the West End cliffs. The menu separates breakfast, lunch and dinner, with Jamaican cooking and vegetarian choices; check the service you want rather than assuming every dish runs all day.

Tony’s call
A lively beach meal makes more sense here than dressing it up as a quiet formal dinner.
Best for
Beach-day groups, Jamaican breakfast and visitors comparing vegetarian choices.
Skip if
Your main priority is a quiet, formal dining room.
Time — planning estimate
45–90 minutes for a casual meal; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Confirm the current breakfast, lunch or dinner service and outside-guest arrival arrangements.
Access
Ask for the route from the entrance to your table, beach-side surfaces, shade and toilets.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
Next stop
Continue your beach block; do not add a distant cliff transfer merely to fill the afternoon.
