Border / Scott’s Cove — fried fish & bammy

Address: Scott’s Cove (Border), St. Elizabeth–Westmoreland boundary, near Whitehouse

Stop at the St. Elizabeth–Westmoreland boundary for fried fish, bammy and festival from a cluster of local cook shops.

Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Border is the local name for Scott’s Cove, where the road crosses between St. Elizabeth and Westmoreland. Choose a cook shop and ask what fish is ready, its size and whether the price covers the fish alone or the sides. Fish with bammy makes this a food stop rooted in local trade, not an interchangeable restaurant listing.

The stalls and the recovery

JIS reported on 30 December 2025 that vendors were returning after Hurricane Melissa destroyed the stalls. The report describes reconstruction of 44 shops, with concrete anchoring and hurricane straps. Buying a meal here connects the visitor economy with cooks, fisherfolk and produce suppliers. That dated recovery report does not confirm every stall’s hours today. Read the JIS recovery report ↗

Tony’s call
Build Border into a Whitehouse–Black River transfer. Make the meal the stop; do not add a cross-island drive simply to collect it.
Best for
Fried or escovitch fish, bammy, festival and a South Coast roadside meal.
Consider another option if
You need a guaranteed opening time, a verified step-free dining room or a fixed menu before travelling.
Time
Allow 30–60 minutes for choosing and eating; editorial estimate, excluding your drive.
Cost
Ask each vendor for the total in JMD before ordering; fish size, sides and preparation affect the price. No current fixed price verified.
Hours & booking
Independent vendors keep their own hours. Ask your host or driver which stalls are operating on your travel day.
Access
Confirm seating, toilet access, curb or steps and a suitable drop-off with the particular stall; no site-wide step-free route verified.
Parking & pickup
Use a safe designated stopping place and agree pickup with your driver; do not stop in moving traffic.
Next stop
Continue toward Whitehouse or Black River according to your route; Middle Quarters is a separate inland food stop.
