JNHT documents Castleton as a botanical garden with an important plant-introduction history. That makes it more than an attractive picnic backdrop: ask how useful plants moved through colonial agricultural networks and whose knowledge shaped their use. Public opening, facilities and current path condition require checking with the responsible authority.

Tony’s Call Choose Castleton for a real inland day, preferably with a guide who knows the plants. Do not mistake “garden” for a guaranteed easy-access or all-weather visit.
Best for
Plant enthusiasts, families prepared for an inland trip and local residents revisiting a familiar place.
Skip if
You have little daylight, heavy weather or only a short coastal window.
Timing
