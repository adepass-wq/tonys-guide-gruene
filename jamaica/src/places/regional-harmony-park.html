Harmony Beach Park

A public waterfront park for walking, sitting and a less structured family stop.

Why make room for it?

UDC describes a public waterfront park with green space and beach access. It adds something important to a travel guide: a place used for ordinary recreation, not only packaged excursions. Beach access and the ability to walk the grounds are different decisions.

Look, listen and ask

Compare who uses the promenade and green space at different times of day. Ask before photographing people. UDC published a September 2026 jogging-track resurfacing tender; that is a reason to ask about works, not proof that the whole park is closed.

Tony’s call
Use the park for a modest outing rather than buying an attraction package when you mainly want open space.
Best for
A waterfront stroll, a picnic or time between other downtown visits.
Skip if
You are relying on a particular play area, swimming service or jogging route being available without checking.
Time — planning estimate
Allow 45–90 minutes for a walk or rest; beach time is a separate choice.
Cost
UDC describes free public access. Budget separately for transport, parking and purchases.
Hours & booking
Confirm current park and swimming hours; individual facilities may have separate availability.
Access
Ask about the entrance-to-promenade route, toilets and any works affecting walking surfaces.
Parking & pickup
Arrange the exact entrance and pickup with your host, driver or venue; directions are destination-based, not a surveyed route.
Next stop
Combine with the Cultural Centre if its opening and your walking/transport plan fit.
