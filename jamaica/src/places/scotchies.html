Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The tourism board identifies Scotchies in Drax Hall as a jerk-food stop. Order with your group’s appetite, spice tolerance and dietary needs in mind rather than treating every “authentic” dish as compulsory. Ask which items are ready now, what portion size means and whether sides are included. This is a practical meal stop, not a booked tasting itinerary.

Tony’s Call Choose it for a straightforward jerk lunch when you are already travelling west. Do not make your whole party eat the same thing to prove they visited Jamaica.
Best for

Accessibility
Ask about entrance surfaces, seating and restroom access. Food allergies require a direct kitchen discussion.

Confirm Drax Hall location, legal parking and drop-off; there are other Scotchies branches.
