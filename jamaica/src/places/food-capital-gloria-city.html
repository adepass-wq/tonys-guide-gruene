Why make room for it?

The operator locates Seafood City at Victoria Pier on Ocean Boulevard and publishes fish, shrimp and other seafood categories. Gloria’s Port Royal reputation is part of the brand’s story, but this is the city waterfront branch. Check the chosen dish, current portion and final bill; the menu’s seafood range does not establish that every seasonal catch is available.
