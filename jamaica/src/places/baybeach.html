NEPA’s beach guide locates Ocho Rios Bay Beach between the marina and the hotel now known as Moon Palace. The shoreline is part of a busy resort town: boats, visitors and commercial activity are part of the setting. Confirm the public entrance, admission and any chair or umbrella charge at the gate. A town beach is a different choice from a resort day pass, and a nearby hotel does not grant access to its private facilities.

Tony’s Call The practical choice when time is short. For a cruise stop, establish your terminal-to-beach route and return deadline before settling in.
Best for
Short stays, cruise visitors and anyone wanting a central swimming stop.
Skip if
You expect isolation or a guaranteed quiet bay without boat activity.
Timing
Allow 1–3 hours according to your return deadline.
Cost
Public recreational access can involve an entrance charge; current admission, rentals and watersports prices need gate confirmation.
Hours & booking
Confirm current gate hours and swimming conditions.
Accessibility
Sand is a mobility barrier; ask about hard-surface approaches, accessible toilets and assistance. Central location does not establish beach wheelchair access.
Parking & arrival
Town parking and terminal routes vary; do not use hotel spaces without permission.
Sensible next stop
Turtle River Park, Ocho Rios Craft Market and a town meal.
