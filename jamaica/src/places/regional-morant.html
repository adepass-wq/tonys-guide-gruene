Why make room for it?

JNHT places the fort behind the courthouse overlooking the harbour and dates its construction only tentatively. That distinction matters: the fort’s physical landscape is evidence, while the precise date requires qualification. Read the Morant Bay rebellion article for the broader conflict rather than asking the ruins to explain it alone.

Look, listen and ask

Compare the roles of courthouse, monument and harbour. What kinds of authority did each represent? Keep to permitted routes and ask locally whether any restoration or restrictions affect the visit.
