The tourism board describes Coward’s home, work spaces and burial at Firefly. The view is a major part of the appeal, but the house is also a way to examine expatriate cultural networks and the labour supporting elite retreats. A photograph of the estate does not establish its current opening or restoration condition. Make contact before committing transport; old guidebook rates and weekly closing days are not reliable substitutes for a current confirmation.

Tony’s Call A strong companion to Port Maria for literary and architectural interests—provided you have confirmed access. A view alone is not a reason to assume the road or interior suits everyone.
Best for
Literature, architecture and visitors wanting coastal views with a story.
