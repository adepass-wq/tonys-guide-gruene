Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The heritage trust identifies Annotto Bay Baptist Church as a historic site. Approach the town as a place people use daily, not a stage set. The church offers a way into education, faith and the history of labour; our town essay connects those subjects to the coast. Interior access, opening and guided interpretation need arrangements.

Tony’s Call Best as a deliberate St. Mary outing. Do not add it to an already overloaded Ochi afternoon just because it sits on a map.
Best for
Church history, local communities and visitors spending more than one day in St. Mary.
Skip if
You need a short Ocho Rios return or unconfirmed interiors are central to your plan.
Timing
Allow 1–2 hours in town plus a driver’s route estimate.
Cost
Public-street observation has no tour ticket; a guide, refreshments and any organised church visit are separate.
Hours & booking
Daylight; request church access ahead and respect services.
Accessibility
Pavements and thresholds are not uniformly accessible. Agree a manageable route and toilet stop with your host.
Parking & arrival
Arrange legal parking and drop-off locally; avoid busy traffic approaches.
Sensible next stop
Castleton only as a separate inland extension with enough daylight and confirmed conditions.
