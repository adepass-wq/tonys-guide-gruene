St. Ann’s Bay & Marcus Garvey

Location: St. Ann’s Bay, Jamaica

A working parish town where Garvey’s birthplace and the waterfront lead into questions about trade, power and remembrance.

Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The heritage trust identifies 32 Market Street as Marcus Garvey’s birthplace. That fact does not mean a visitor can enter the property without an appointment. Build a daylight town visit around public streets and agreed access, then use our Garvey and Seville histories for context. Look at the relationship between civic buildings, businesses, the waterfront and everyday movement rather than treating the whole town as a museum.

Tony’s Call Combine the town with Seville on a weekday. Go for the town’s story, not a promise of unrestricted access to Garvey’s birthplace.
