Le Vendôme at Charela Inn

Jamaican–French cooking for an unhurried beach-strip meal.

Explore this region →
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Why make room for it?

Charela’s restaurant combines Jamaican and French cooking, with a changing five-course dinner and à la carte choices. The operator links its food supply to Cocoa Farm. That makes the meal a useful encounter with western farming as well as hotel dining, without promising that a sample menu is tonight’s menu.

Tony’s call
Choose this for a meal you give time to, not a quick stop between excursions.
Best for
An unhurried dinner and diners interested in the farm-to-table connection.
Skip if
You need a fast roadside turnaround.
Time — planning estimate
90–120 minutes for dinner; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Reserve and check the current five-course and à la carte options, outside-guest access and dietary arrangements.
Access
Ask about the route into the dining room and to toilets, plus any beach-side steps.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
Next stop
Stay on the beach-strip branch and protect the time needed for the meal.
