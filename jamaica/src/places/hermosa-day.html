Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Hermosa Cove advertises a non-resident beach/pool day pass from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. Kayaking and snorkeling are optional extras. Arrange entry in advance, confirm currency and the total with supplements, and ask about conditions and equipment. Outside food and liquor are not allowed.
