A guided limestone-cave visit between the Runaway Bay and Discovery Bay areas; pair geology with a west-coast day.

Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The former Green Grotto website now directs visitors to Chukka’s attraction page. Use that current operator route rather than an unofficial ticket site with a similar name. Expect a guided underground visit, not an open-access cavern to wander alone. Darkness, confined passages and uneven surfaces are part of the decision. Historical stories associated with the caves deserve careful interpretation; a tour anecdote is not automatically evidence for a specific escape or battle.

Tony’s Call A worthwhile change from beaches and falls. Keep it on a western St. Ann route, with Puerto Seco as the natural companion.
Best for
Geology, caves and visitors wanting an experience that differs from another waterfall.
Skip if
Confined spaces, low light or steps are a serious obstacle for your group.
Timing
Allow 1–1½ hours for the visit, plus check-in; editorial estimate.
Cost
Paid guided tour. Current Chukka booking supplies the price and inclusions; older US$20 references are not presented as today’s guaranteed rate.
Hours & booking
Check current tour slots and last admission through the operator.
Accessibility
Ask about stairs, helmets, low passages and suitability for children or limited mobility; do not assume wheelchair access.
Parking & arrival
Confirm the main-road entrance and parking with the operator or driver.
Sensible next stop
Puerto Seco Beach; Sharkies in Salem on your return toward Ocho Rios.
