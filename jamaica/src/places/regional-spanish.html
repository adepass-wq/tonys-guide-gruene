Why make room for it?

JNHT records that Spanish Town remained the capital after the English capture in 1655 until Kingston became the capital in 1872. The buildings around the square help you investigate how colonial government displayed authority, but the story also includes the people governed from this place.

Look, listen and ask

Read the Spanish Town article before the outing. Pick one building and ask what function it served, who could enter and how that changed. Heritage status and present-day admission should always be checked separately.
