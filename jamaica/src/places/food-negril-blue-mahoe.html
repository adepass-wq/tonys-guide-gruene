Why make room for it?

JTB identifies Blue Mahoe at Ocean Cliff Hotel and currently lists it open. Use it as a genuine alternative when comparing the West End’s restaurant settings and table availability. Choose the confirmed meal service and seating area; a tourism listing is not a live menu or a reservation.

Compare it with Ivan’s and Rockhouse, then choose one restaurant that fits your base and confirmed booking.

90–120 minutes for dinner; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Confirm current menu, service, outside-guest arrival and reservation directly with Ocean Cliff.
Access
Ask about cliffside steps and the entrance-to-table route, rather than assuming hotel dining is accessible.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
