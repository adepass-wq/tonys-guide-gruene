Hours & booking
Daylight town visit; private venues and beach access need separate confirmation.
Accessibility
Road edges and pavements vary. Plan a short, shaded route with your driver rather than assuming continuous accessible sidewalks.
Parking & arrival
Agree a legal pickup point in town; private property entrances are not public parking.
