Ocho Rios Craft Market

A town shopping stop where buying thoughtfully matters more than buying quickly.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The tourism board lists Ocho Rios Craft Market among open attractions. Ask a seller who made an item, what materials it uses and whether its story is a documented one or a sales narrative. Agree a price and currency before paying, and leave room in your bag for fragile work. Photograph people only with permission. This is not a free cultural performance staged for visitors: it is a place where people are trying to earn a living.

Tony’s Call Buy something you actually value, and ask good questions. A respectful conversation is better than treating every stall as a competition to win the lowest price.
Best for
Handmade gifts, short town visits and visitors who want direct conversations with vendors.
Skip if
You dislike active selling or are rushing to meet a ship deadline.
Timing
Allow 30–60 minutes; editorial estimate.
Cost
Purchases vary. Decide your spending limit in JMD or USD and confirm payment method before bargaining.
Hours & booking
Confirm current trading hours, especially on holidays or late afternoons.
Accessibility
Aisles and stall thresholds vary; ask about a route suitable for your group.
Parking & arrival
Use legal town parking or an agreed driver pickup; keep your return time visible.
Sensible next stop
Turtle River Park and Ocho Rios Bay Beach.
