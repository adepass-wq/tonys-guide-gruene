Why make room for it?

Rockhouse Restaurant is a dining choice within the cliffside hotel, not a beach-swimming destination. Its meal services let you choose a daytime lunch or a reserved evening rather than treating sunset as the only reason to come. Pushcart is a separate restaurant on the same property, with a different atmosphere.

Tony’s call
Let the meal anchor the evening. Reserve the time and setting you actually want instead of collecting several sunset stops.
Best for
A deliberate sit-down meal and a cliffside setting.
Skip if
You want to combine lunch with easy sandy-beach entry.
Time — planning estimate
90–120 minutes for a reserved dinner; editorial estimate.
Cost
Use the current menu and confirm currency, portions, taxes and service charges. Seafood may be priced by weight; ask for the total before ordering.
Hours & booking
Ask which service admits outside guests, whether a reservation is required and the current menu.
Access
Request the actual route to your reserved table, steps and toilet access.
Parking & pickup
Confirm the entrance and where your driver can wait or collect you; avoid assuming the beach-side entrance has vehicle access.
Next stop
Use a West End return route; the Seven Mile Beach strip is a separate branch.
