Read Tony’s guide & practical details

Konoko combines gardens and a waterfall experience in the hills above Ocho Rios. Treat it as a distinct attraction, not a cheaper version of Dunn’s River. Decide whether your group is coming for gardens, interpretation or a climb, and buy the appropriate visit. Online listings disagree about opening days; the tourism board’s open status is useful but does not settle Monday or Tuesday access. Call before building a day around it.

Tony’s Call A useful alternative when you want greenery and views close to town. Do not promise an easy-access outing simply because it has gardens.
Best for
Garden visitors and groups wanting nature without travelling far inland.
Skip if
You have a tight return deadline and have not confirmed the day’s opening.
Timing
Allow 1½–2½ hours on site; planning estimate.
Cost
Paid attraction; current standalone admission and local rates require an operator quote. A tour-company transfer price is not the gate price.
Hours & booking
Confirm the exact date with Konoko; conflicting published weekly schedules remain unresolved.
Accessibility
Hillside routes and waterfall steps; ask separately about garden access, restrooms and avoiding the climb.
Parking & arrival
Arrange transport up to the attraction and confirm accessible drop-off; do not assume an easy walk from the bay.
Sensible next stop
Turtle River Park and a town meal.
