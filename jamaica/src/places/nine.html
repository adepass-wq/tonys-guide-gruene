Nine Mile / Bob Marley Mausoleum

A deliberate inland music pilgrimage, not a stop to tack onto a short Ocho Rios cruise visit.

Directions ↗
Read Tony’s guide & practical details

The 9 Mile Reggae Land operator describes guided access to Marley’s birthplace, memorabilia, murals and the mausoleum. Connect the visit to the rural community and the journey to Kingston that shaped the broader music story. The drive into the hills is a substantial part of the outing; allow a driver’s realistic estimate instead of assuming the map’s distance equals travel time. Respect the chapel and burial context as well as the attraction’s visitor character.

Tony’s Call Go if Marley’s life and rural Jamaica matter to you. On a short visit, choose Nine Mile or a coastal adventure rather than trying to force both into one afternoon.
Best for
Reggae listeners, diaspora visitors and travellers prepared for an inland drive.
Skip if
You have a narrow all-aboard window or are sensitive to winding-road journeys.
Timing
Budget roughly half a day including transport; confirm route time with your driver.
Cost
Paid guided site visit; obtain the current age/residency rate and clarify whether transport and food are included.
Hours & booking
Confirm tour hours and booking arrangements through 9 Mile Reggae Land.
Accessibility
Ask about steps, walking length, chapel access and vehicle drop-off before booking.
Parking & arrival
Use the operator’s destination and agreed driver pickup; this is not an independently verified step-free walk.
Sensible next stop
Lunch on the return route; use our music atlas before or after the visit.
