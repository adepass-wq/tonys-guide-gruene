Why make room for it?

Café Blue’s Irish Town location is identified in JTB’s mountain-coffee guide, while the operator publishes its cafe collection and menu. Choose this branch when the mountain road is already part of your day. It is a different destination from Café Blue in Ocho Rios or the city, and a cafe stop is not a coffee-farm tour.

Tony’s call
Use it for a coffee-and-meal break on an Irish Town route, with room for the drive.
