# Tony’s Guide Jamaica staging audit

Audited: the 90-article staging checkout, following the fifth parish batch. Production was not used as the audit target. Nothing published or synchronized remotely.

## Decision

The corrected staging version is stronger, but is **not cleared for publication**. Desktop/mobile browser inspection and audible player checks were not available through the supported workflow. Image reuse permissions remain unresolved for several source collections. These are explicit verification gaps, not proof that the layout or media fails.

The fifteen-per-parish programme remains incomplete: 56 dedicated parish narratives, 25 islandwide articles and nine shared comparisons; every parish has at least three dedicated articles. Proposed subjects and learning-outing cards are not counted as completed parish narratives.

## Evidence

- The source/generated inventory remains 90 articles. All 90 have learning-path membership. Thirteen pathways and 33 arranged heritage outings remain available.
- Static inventory found no unresolved internal anchor targets in the baseline assembled page. Runtime navigation is checked separately in DOM tests.
- Eleven exact repeated paragraph groups were identified. Most are repeated recording context, attribution or source-image captions. Their necessary context was retained; this count is not a measure of unique substantive prose or a complete similarity analysis.
- All 68 unique inline image assets decoded successfully (raster verification or SVG XML parsing). This establishes valid embedded data, not browser display, visual quality, historical accuracy or reproduction rights.
- Thirty of those 68 images are narrower than 400 pixels. They can serve as small documentary illustrations, but limit enlargement and visual polish. Higher-resolution originals should be sought without inventing or replacing historical details.
- A bounded external-resource sample covered 22 image/audio/player endpoints: fourteen returned data, eight retrievals failed. Failures included HTTP429, gateway/incomplete responses and proxy403 denials. These do not establish public unavailability. No third-party stream was heard. See `audit/external-resource-health.json`.
- Source descriptions and image date/location labels were inspected. This pass did not independently re-research every factual sentence in all 90 articles. Earlier explicit source conflicts remain documented rather than silently harmonised.
- No visitor-facing TODO/FIXME or staged-first-draft labels were found in article prose. Legitimate source/date/access caveats remain.

## Candid assessment

| Dimension | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Content | Stronger breadth and substantially deeper than the original snippets. Source depth remains uneven: some articles contain detailed documentary/archaeological evidence; others depend heavily on brief institutional summaries and learning questions. Continue local archival and community research. |
| Navigation | Functional structure and automated journey evidence are strong, with the principal discovery-order problem corrected. Do not call it visually optimised until real screens are inspected. |
| Engagement | Notes, export, reading progress, exercises, pathways and recordings provide meaningful learning functions. Image resolution and text-heavy sections still limit the presentation; actual playback remains an open check. |
| Mobile usability | Responsive rules and controls were refined, but this is a code-level assessment only. No defensible visual mobile rating is possible from this environment. |
| Performance | The site remains a roughly 9.1 MB uncompressed, self-contained HTML build. Card batching reduces initial DOM duplication; it does not reduce that transfer size. Measure loading and interaction on a real phone before calling the system optimised. Consider separating assets as a future coordinated build/hosting change. |

## Required release review

Inspect this exact corrected build on desktop and a narrow mobile screen. Complete search → parish filter → article → note → mark read → export → next article → local outing journeys. Actually play the 1921 Indiana/Garvey samples, representative BBC/Spotify/YouTube resources, and regional recordings; test route departure, global stop and backgrounding. Review loading failures and record whether they are provider/access restrictions or broken resources. Resolve image reproduction permissions, particularly publisher-attributed JNHT and owner-site photographs for which no open licence was established. Check the largest and smallest images, long citations, menu, current conditions and initial load time. Then fix verified issues and rerun affected checks before a publication decision.

## Final validation and saved output

The final `npm run release:prepare` completed successfully: **28 tests passed, zero failed**, in 189.3 seconds, using a 4 GB worker heap and concurrency two. These include three new complete automated journeys for discovery/filter recovery, stored-note export/malformed-link recovery and conditions-cache/refresh failure. Syntax, structured data, article/source inventory, IDs, citations and image descriptions passed. An earlier run exposed malformed-fragment exceptions in two older modules; those were corrected before the successful full rerun.

Generated HTML: 90 articles, 333 source modules, 9,095,985 bytes. SHA256: `594894ce0bb3241beb9e725bedcb60a77e222bec298e16dc5f083f082e4fe31d`. The saved package's CRC, 90-article metadata and exact HTML-byte match were checked. This audit report and the two verification ledgers are included in the package. No desktop/mobile screenshots or audible-player checks are claimed. No publication or remote synchronization.
