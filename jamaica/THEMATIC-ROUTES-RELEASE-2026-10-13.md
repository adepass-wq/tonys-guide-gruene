# Ten thematic Jamaica routes

Scope: 10 named route guides, 40 ordered stop entries, every one of the eight travel regions. The Kingston region has separate city/mountain routes; St. Ann and St. Mary have separate routes. Remaining regions each have one route. Longer itineraries are split into local days. Stops have activities, transfer notes, source links and related scholarship-based reading. Routes have walking, driving and public-transport guidance, access alternatives, cost categories, credited existing photographs and day map links.

Source: `src/data/thematic-routes.json`; build renderer: `scripts/thematic-routes.cjs`. The generator preserves existing place photographs and captions. No new stock image or synthetic historical scene was created. The collection is linked from Home, Regions, History and Plan, indexed in full-text search and filterable by region/theme/query. Deep links open the relevant route and clear conflicting local route filters.

Research on 13 October 2026: Jamaica National Heritage Trust site/parish/historic-district pages; National Gallery official visitor page; JCDT/Holywell park information; Jamaica Tourism Board transport/food/place pages; Knutsford Express booking/contact/location pages; JUTC route guide; UDC Harmony Beach Park; existing place-operator sources and linked guide essays. Operator pages establish the contact or service type, not booked access on a visitor's date. Route order, duration allowances and learning questions are editorial recommendations. No invented bus number, departure time, fare or through-ticket is published.

Limits: some external URLs returned 403, timed out or returned a server error in direct HTTP checks; accessible search copies corroborated several, including UDC and JTB. Bath's official directory could not be fetched in the fresh check; the route requires direct service confirmation. Coach office hours are not represented as departure times. On-site walking surfaces, current road conditions, bathing suitability and actual departures are not independently field-tested.

Verification: structural/syntax validation; functional route coverage, all internal route links, deep links, filter recovery and search tests; full existing release checks. Three assertions inherited from the unchanged remote storytelling release were stale (menu now has seven primary destinations; written historical tales link to resources instead of embedding media). Baseline inspection confirmed these conditions before those assertions were updated to validate current contracts.

Local preview: server launches, but this tool environment cannot reach its loopback listener from the separate browser/network context. Existing automated preview test starts the server and verifier together. Live HTML equality and live browser verification are required after deployment.
