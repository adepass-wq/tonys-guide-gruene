# Jamaican Foodways — comprehensive 107-entry authentication screening

Date: 2026-10-16. Branch: staging/jamaica-foodways-2026-10-15. STAGING ONLY; NO PRODUCTION RELEASE.

## Meaning and limitations
This is a **full-inventory documentary screening**, NOT full recipe authentication. Every one of the 107 staged recipe records was examined for title, ingredients, method availability, source attribution, and safety review needs. Independent research checked the named institutional sources below; it did not inspect all Jamaican published cookbooks or conduct 107 kitchen trials. **Absence from these sources is not evidence a dish does not exist.** No staged formula currently has documented source provenance linking its exact proportions and steps to a Jamaican cook; none is kitchen-tested. Thus **0/107 exact staged formulations can be certified**. This does NOT invalidate the documented existence of many dishes.

## High-priority corrections
1. Remove the count of 107 recipes from public-facing language until attribution and testing support publication.
2. **Ackee and saltfish:** JTB chef-attributed ingredient list differs from our staging quantities and should be cited rather than falsely claiming our formula follows that chef. Use only food-safe commercially processed ackee for accessible home instructions.
3. **Cassava/bammy:** staged formulas employ varying cassava products; never treat untreated bitter cassava as safe without validated processing.
4. **Kidney beans:** independently verify soak/boil instructions and cooking temperatures in rice-and-peas or stew-peas before publishing.
5. **Jerk:** authentic spice components are documented; precise proportions and method of our recipes are not validated.
6. **Coconut drops breakfast variation:** existence of coconut drops is documented, but the breakfast categorization is editorial and should not imply traditional breakfast usage.
7. **Duplicates/near-duplicates:** bammy vs cassava bammy; escovitch fish vs escovitch fish with bammy; saltfish fritters vs stamp-and-go; Ital stew vs Ital vegetable stew; do not count combinations as independent historical traditions without documentation.
8. **History:** no recipe-specific colonial-era origin chronology is inferred from present-day cooking or JCDC competition category alone. Historical essays require pinpoint citations.

## Publication gate for each recipe
Record source author/cook and locale, first publication/recording date, exact supporting ingredient proportions and method, copyright treatment (paraphrase without copied expressive instructions), evidence of actual preparation, family/parish variation attribution, kitchen trial or explicit untested label, food safety checks, and real authorized photography. Only then move an entry from research inventory to publication review. Institutional dish recognition alone is inadequate.
