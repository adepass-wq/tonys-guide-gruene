Date: 2026-10-16

## Distinct evidence levels
- **D1**: Jamaican dish identified by institution or identifiable community cooking venue. Confirms dish identity only.
- **R1**: An attributable published recipe supplies specific ingredient quantities AND steps. This applies to the *published source recipe*, not to a different Tony's Guide formulation.
- **M1**: Tony's Guide's proposed formula independently cross-checked line by line against a specific source, with deviations marked. No such approval should be presumed.
- **T1**: Independently cooked/tested, results and dates recorded. NONE has this level in this staging branch.

## Authenticity issues and next acquisition steps
1. Prior 107-entry screen was a **limited institutional index**, not full verification. Callaloo omelette is now independently established as a named Jamaican menu item; adjust future aggregate counts accordingly, but do not overstate other classifications.
2. Do not publish text with the implication that a JCDC *dish list* validates any recipe proportions. The JCDC handbook has a separate recipe-writing category, but is not itself a source of all listed recipes.
3. For each remaining item seek at least one attributable full recipe (author, title, date, URL/page, exact formula) and ideally independent corroboration from another Jamaican cook. Record differences rather than choose a supposedly universal traditional method.
4. Obtain consent if recording family and community recipes; preserve original phrasing without copying protected prose from commercial publications.
5. Mark present-day dishes, family adaptations, and historically attested practices separately. Never assign a parish origin without evidence.
6. Independently test recipe usability and food safety before calling any formula tested or publish-ready.
