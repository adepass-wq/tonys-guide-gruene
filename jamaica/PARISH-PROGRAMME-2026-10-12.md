# Parish history and culture programme

Target: at least 15 substantial, dedicated articles for each of Jamaica’s 14 parishes: 210 parish-specific articles minimum. National stories, incidental mentions, shared comparisons and activity cards do not count toward a dedicated parish minimum. Existing local articles may satisfy a proposed subject after substantive editorial review.

Status: 90 staged articles overall, including thirty-six new local drafts. The 210-subject plan is an editorial inventory, not a completed collection. Every parish has a dedicated narrative now; none has reached the 15-article goal by the strict primary-parish count. New drafts contain roughly 600–950 source-text words each, including captions and notes; these counts are not a quality score. They should be developed further with local archival records, additional current/historical media, and community sources.

## Standards

Each complete article needs a distinct local question, substantial historical narrative, named places and people, evidence tied to claims, relevant inline images with real location/date/credit, a learning task, connected reading and a practical activity where appropriate. Audio/video belongs where it contributes to the subject; unrelated clips do not satisfy a media quota. Source availability and media playback are separate checks.
